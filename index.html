Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I play if blooket1.com is blocked at my school?

Click Stealth Mode → Mirrors & Alt Links in the top bar to copy our official alternate domains and educational paths, including blooket1.pages.dev, educational.blooket1.com, blooket1.com/drive.google.com/, and blooket1.com/classroom.google.com/.

How does about:blank tab cloaking work?

Click Open in about:blank in the top bar or inside Stealth Mode. Blooket1 will open inside a clean about:blank tab that hides the URL from your address bar and browser history while sending the original tab to Google Classroom.

What is the Panic Key (Boss Key)?

Press the backtick key (`) at the top-left of your keyboard at any time to immediately replace your game tab with Google Classroom, Google Docs, or Wikipedia. You can customize your Panic Key inside Stealth Mode.

Does my game progress save on school Chromebooks?

Yes! Your favorite games, recently played list, Stealth Mode disguise, and in-game saves (for games like Retro Bowl, Cookie Clicker, and Brotato) are stored automatically in your browser's local storage.
