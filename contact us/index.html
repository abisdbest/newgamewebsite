DMCA & Copyright Takedown Notices

Blooket1 respects the intellectual property rights of game developers, publishers, and artists. In accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)), we will swiftly process and respond to notices of alleged copyright infringement.

To file a valid DMCA Notice, please email support@blooket1.com with the subject line [DMCA Takedown Request] <Game Name> and include:

  • A description of the copyrighted work that you claim has been infringed.
  • The exact URL on Blooket1 (e.g. https://blooket1.pages.dev/play/.../) where the allegedly infringing material is located.
  • Your contact information: full name, organization (if applicable), mailing address, phone number, and official email address.
  • A statement by you that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement by you, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  • An electronic or physical signature of the person authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

Upon receipt of a valid and complete DMCA notice, we will act expeditiously to remove or disable access to the infringing material within 24–48 hours.
