Duck Life 4: The Ultimate Training Adventure

Duck Life 4 is an acclaimed training and racing adventure game. After the ban on genetically modified ducks, it's up to you to raise and train your own champion duck from the ground up.

Advertisement

Compete across six distinct training realms including Grassland, Swamp, Mountains, Glacier, City, and Volcano. Train running, flying, swimming, and climbing to level up your stats and unlock prestigious race tournaments.

Collect coins, buy stylish hats and color customizations, recruit a team of specialized ducks, and conquer every league to become the world champion racer.

How to play Duck Life 4

  1. Pick a training minigame for running, swimming, flying or climbing. Each one raises that skill.
  2. Use the arrow keys (or your mouse) to dodge obstacles and collect coins while you train.
  3. Training uses energy. Spend coins at the shop on food to top it back up.
  4. When your duck is ready, enter the area's race. Win to unlock the next area and a tougher league.

Tips for winning races

  • Races mix several terrains, so train every skill instead of maxing just one.
  • Replay the minigames you're best at to farm coins quickly.
  • Check which terrain a race uses most before entering, and train that skill first.
  • Hats and colors are cosmetic. Spend on food first, then customize.
