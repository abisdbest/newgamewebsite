Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Welcome to Blooket1 (accessible via blooket1.pages.dev and blooket1.com). At Blooket1, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Blooket1 and how we use it.

1. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

2. Information We Collect

Blooket1 does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, home address, or phone number to play games. Any game preferences, favorite games, or custom settings (like tab cloaking and theme choices) are stored solely on your local device via browser localStorage and never sent to our servers.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

Google is one of our third-party advertising vendors. Google uses cookies, specifically the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to blooket1 and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Analytics & Log Files

Blooket1 uses standard log files and Google Analytics (Measurement ID: G-VNGZND6VMN). These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. This data is used solely to analyze trends, administer the site, and improve user gameplay experiences.

5. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Blooket1 uses 'cookies' to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

6. GDPR Data Protection Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), European Union residents are entitled to the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, or transfer their data. Because Blooket1 does not collect personally identifying information or user accounts, no personal databases are maintained. For any inquiries, contact our team below.

7. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, request deletion of data, and opt out of the sale of personal information. Blooket1 does not sell personal information to third parties.

8. Children's Information (COPPA)

Protecting children while using the internet is especially important to us. Blooket1 does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately.

9. Contact Information

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through email at support@blooket1.com or visit our Contact Us page.
